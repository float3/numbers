Number games

For a room of people

Everyone picks a number

You pick a rule and a range. Everyone picks a number on their phone. The rule decides who wins.

The rules

  • Closest to a fraction of the average: a third, a half, two thirds, or the average itself.
  • Closest to the same fraction of the middle number.
  • The lowest number nobody else picked. Whole numbers, and some rounds have no winner.
  • The highest number nobody else picked.
  • The highest number below the average.
  • The number the most people picked. Nobody wins if no two people pick the same number.
  • The number furthest from everybody else's, measured to the nearest other pick.
  • The highest number, if everybody's numbers together stay under a limit you set. Over the limit, nobody wins.
